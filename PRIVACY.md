# AnyDeck Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 2026_

AnyDeck is designed to work without collecting personal data.

- **Everything stays on your device.** Scores, statistics, settings, coins, and the decks
  you create are stored locally and never uploaded.
- **No accounts.** The app has no sign-up, login, or user identifiers.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app contains no analytics SDKs and no ad tracking.
- **AI deck generation.** When you generate a deck, only the topic text, language, and
  card count you enter are sent to our server, which forwards them to Google's Gemini API
  to produce the deck. This text is not linked to you and is not stored by us.
- **Sharing.** Deck codes and QR codes are created on your device and shared only through
  the apps you choose.

Questions: open an issue on the project repository.
